# Align the key-keeper summary with generation fencing and D-IDENTITY

- **Lens:** adversarial trust contract; identities and lifecycle consistency.
- **Problem:** The trust contract still describes key-keeper registration as a mutable UID-to-path map and permits dispatcher revocation at retirement without the established one-shot restriction. The companion module contracts require generation-bound registration, active-ledger checks and detachment before UID reuse, and distinguish account retirement from identity revocation. Following the trust summary alone permits a recycled UID to reach an old identity mapping or normal standing-account retirement to revoke a persistent identity. This is an inconsistent specification, not an enabled or demonstrated exploit: keeper integration remains gated on publication and qualification.
- **Evidence:**
  - `swarm-platform-architecture@6b3f9831f9c928cd316e2d1886a0e1c89465af20`, `vision/contracts/trust-and-isolation.md:17-19`: `register(uid, path)`, mutable `UID -> path`, `revoke(identity)` by the owner or dispatcher at retire, and retirement equated with a root-signed revocation record.
  - `swarma-dispatcher@19a75e63cd794f93c81c8c6fdb9d176eba1ed87c`, `spec/dispatcher.md:596-599`: keeper integration requires `(uid, generation, identity path, owner, registration generation)`, the one-shot flag recorded at registration, active-ledger checks and detachment before UID reuse. Only the dispatcher that registered a one-shot identity may revoke it, as a separate journaled effect after confirmed account retirement; standing identities require an operator-signed revocation. The integration is disabled until its API and migration procedure exist.
  - `swarma-user-manager@ae540c596c9416f1592491fc6133c36538c41921`, `spec/helper.md:208`, and `swarma-credential-broker@393b724f66a8b4c6f8cb55a163d6eb9e2fda6548`, `spec/broker.md:181`, explicitly preserve the same D-IDENTITY distinction.
  - `wiki@87f387d1109fb41213394a8d794dfbbbaefacba6`, `book/swarma/ru/04-klyuchi.md:39-54`, already describes the generation-bound registration, active-ledger check and separate one-shot/standing lifecycle; `decisions.md:589` records D-IDENTITY.
- **Proposed change:** Update trust-contract §2 to state and reference the existing keeper-integration gate: generation-bound registration with ownership and registration identity, active-ledger validation, and detachment before UID reuse. Distinguish account retirement/detachment from identity revocation. Restrict dispatcher revocation to its recorded one-shot identities after confirmed retirement; preserve standing identity continuity until an operator-signed revocation. Keep the detailed API unpublished until the existing joint publication/qualification gate is satisfied. Do not invent a second registration schema.
- **Expected benefit:** The trust summary, book and module integration contracts direct implementers to the same identity boundary and do not accidentally authorize stale-UID signing or loss of persistent identities.
- **Risks and costs:** A small documentation alignment and cross-reference audit. The correction does not claim the keeper is implemented, define an additional wire format or change the owner decision.
- **Affected specs:** `swarm-platform-architecture/vision/contracts/trust-and-isolation.md` §2; its linked identity/retirement descriptions should be checked for the same stale shorthand. Dispatcher §14, user-manager §4.4, broker §4.3 and book chapter 4 provide the already-correct contract.
