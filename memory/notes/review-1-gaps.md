| 2.1 Scope of `complete` | §4: completeness is over "the registered authorized graph", "not a complete account of the world"; field `dependency_coverage: registered_only`; §10: top-k retrieval is not the denominator for contrary-evidence search | Name two separate fields: evaluation completeness over registered data, and **corpus coverage** of the source material (default `unknown`, or `declared` by the reviewer who registered the record). Add a vector: three registered grounds are evaluated, the source holds a fourth unregistered ground, and the result is `complete` + coverage `unknown`, never "all grounds". Separate candidate retrieval from witness evaluation in the API. |
| 2.2 Claim identity and conflict | §3: closed predicate schemas with exact equality; natural-language "same meaning" is not identity; `equivalent` does not merge; a functional-uniqueness predicate may *propose* a contradiction check, scoped to overlapping valid time; S10 | Conflict is defined only as W+ and W- on one exact claim revision. **Incompatibility between different claims** needs closed per-schema rules: subject, scope, valid time, statement kind (fact, proposal, adopted decision), and the incompatibility condition. Two competing proposals are not a factual conflict. An LLM "possible conflict" is a `semantic_review` assessment. Start with 2–3 domain schemas for the pilot (architecture decision, superseded rule, registered assumption). |
| 2.3 Revision order | Design: source-owned sequence sets import order, not wall-clock; adopted status resolves to the deciding authority's receipt. §6: `replace` needs the external authority's supersession evidence; `correct` keeps learned-at | No rule and no vector for **two competing replacements of A** or for **arrival order ≠ decision order**. Proposed rule: the current decision is a projection of the deciding authority's own ordered record (git commit order on the decision register, the board's revision); memory-side replacements without such a receipt stay proposals; two receipted replacements without an order between them are reported as `ambiguous_current` and never resolved by recorded time. New vectors S28 (competing replacements), S29 (late arrival of an earlier decision). |
| 2.4 Qualified support reaches the consumer | §4 per-polarity record carries `evidence_qualification`; S03 expects "B+ with conflicted-premise provenance" | The compact View has no closed field for it. Extend S03 to assert the compact form, not only the explain graph. |
| 2.5 Hidden data in the whole computation | Fully covered: §4 "logical query/evaluation accounting counts authorized work only; hidden candidates scanned before filtering cannot consume that logical budget"; S13, S24, S27.
