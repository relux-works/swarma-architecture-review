## Holds, delivery pauses and redesign

Use three distinct decisions:

- **Hold the affected operation or activation** when a required contract, authorization, implementation, capability row or result is absent, stale, contradictory or unknown. Retain identities, effects and obligations. Protected observation, reconciliation and already-admitted narrowing use their existing contracts; independent work can continue within its own gates.
- **Pause new production work** when a shared provider window, reviewer, runner, landing lane, retention capacity or operator attention cannot support the work already admitted. Preserve current work and reserve its review, repair and qualification capacity. Reforecast or narrow the scope through the existing task-owner workflow. This is scheduling policy, not another broker predicate or permission to expire standing sessions.
- **Redesign the selected mechanism** when a reproducible counterexample, incompatible runtime behavior or demonstrated platform constraint shows that it cannot preserve a required invariant. A missing lab authorization, an ordinary qualification delay or expected retained export debt alone does not establish this. Identify the failing boundary and clean alternatives before changing it; do not add a bypass to manufacture a pass.

The action follows the actual first consumer, not a later milestone label. W may be inside S0a, S0b or board-free H. T adds board import/review/verification/landing. A requires the completed Claude-and-Codex MVP, S2 and T plus its remote tuple. H has its actual tag, authenticated-state/runner and v1 service gates even without a board. Existing-runtime SH1 C1 remains distinct from future platform migration; a combined migration takes both gate sets.

## Accepted throughput and operator attention

Review, qualification, landing and protected operator acts are schedule resources. Growing queues, a second full rework cycle, repeated enrolment/recovery, false watcher incidents or a provider window without repair reserve are early signals to pause new producers and reforecast. Follow the existing task-owner budget/scope process; do not add a quota daemon, exact-spend claim or model change to erase work already in flight.

Measure a complete accepted-change series: trusted context/input-pack preparation, production, eligible independent review, reserved rework, qualification interpretation, close-out, runner queues, retention/transfer and operator time. Keep actual provider/account windows separate from tokens and money; two hosts do not create two allowances. Registry presence is not admitted/available capacity. The reviewed pilot proposes one bounded loop plus the next two comparable changes; report the observed range/max before a scaled forecast, not an invented percentile or price.

For first A, propose one worker and task class until the complete operator/rework load is observed. This is a staging recommendation, not a new admission protocol or adopted numeric ceiling. Expected export refusal and explicit per-act decisions remain legitimate; never promise automatic reclamation to hide their cost. Redesign the workflow when evidence shows a required bounded scope cannot preserve its control/review obligations within the actual owner-accepted resource boundary. A missing measurement or temporary provider refusal first calls for a hold/reforecast, not redesign or weaker review.

## Closing a hold and requalifying a changed mechanism

Record the failed claim, exact source/released-artifact and host/runtime pins, expected versus observed effect, current authority/fences, retained obligations and responsible owner. Do not convert missing implementation or unexecuted cases into a completed slice. A fix names the affected producer/consumer and evidence rows; requalify those rows on the selected tuple and independently review the actual artifacts before activation. Reuse vector authoring and fixtures without carrying unrelated platform/provider passes.

Reopening needs the published current verdict and recovery contract at the effect boundary. Positive delivery evidence resolves that delivery only; it clears no unrelated restore, ALLSTOP, watcher or lifecycle hold. Root replacement recovers broker authorization only. A compatible binary rollback preserves later records. An operator window ends with qualified readiness or an explicit held state and next reconciliation action, never with unknown relabelled success.

No calendar, token, byte or retry kill threshold is invented here. Contract caps remain the actual bounds. Set measurable pilot/forecast bounds with their owners; invariant failure already holds its affected release. Maintain the current board until C1, keep heavy work off the busy operator host, and leave independent qualified or documentation work moving during the hold.
