## Host sequence and the first usable system

Prefer a disposable Linux lab for the first integrated control/recovery implementation, with hosted macOS qualification in parallel. OVH is the candidate for later untrusted execution and verification, not an already qualified host. Select the actual OS, unit, filesystem, network and runtime tuple from measured capability evidence, naming the deployment boundary and its complete service inventory. Calling an existing machine or container “Linux” or “ephemeral” discharges neither R-SHARED nor socket/containment qualification. Neither OS name is an admission rule. Keep the overloaded operator Mac mini on its current working runtime until a qualified replacement and an explicit maintenance window exist; do not use it for heavy builds, candidate execution or destructive qualification.

## Bring-up: control availability before forward readiness

Install the static UID-range denial and pass active precedence probes **before** agent account creation. Install the protected `swarma-operator` and read-only `swarma-observer`; neither is a harness identity. Publish and qualify the phone's pinned-key, exact-act path and the terminal fallback with a fresh password per act. Phone absence must not remove recovery access; an authenticated SSH session alone must not become widening authority. Operator credential custody and proof of the actual act belong to the same first-slice gate, not a later usability project.

Bring up exclusive state owners with known-compatible readers/writers, authenticated sockets and protected evidence paths. Replay effects and validate authentication/indexes before exposing recovery/control. Forward admission additionally requires complete accounting and classification of relevant records, a qualified host/profile tuple and current class/time/revocation evidence. Confined record-local uncertainty may fence that record; shared uncertainty closes node admission. A process accepting connections is not readiness. An absent dispatcher refuses; there is no sudo/command-mode bypass.

Publish one protected operational status view before asking the operator to rely on the platform. It should distinguish control availability from new-work admission, show restore and ALLSTOP fences separately, name unresolved account/effect or writer outcomes, and state which action can reconcile them. Observer reads expose no lease material and cannot mutate authority. A status-only first notification stage is acceptable only when explicit: do not promise unattended phone alerts until a named sender, allowed route and bounded delivery/status contract are qualified. Failed notification never delays narrowing or containment.

## Maintenance and cut-over procedure

The following is the platform R-MAINT procedure for services actually admitted under those contracts. A library-only **SH1 C1 uses the existing board/host maintenance and replacement controls**, as specified separately below; it does not acquire a dependency on future R-OPERATOR, dispatcher/helper/ledger or SH2 readiness merely by sharing a checklist. An explicitly combined platform/C1 migration names and satisfies both sets of gates conjunctively.

For platform maintenance, the exact platform/unit R-FATE table determines the affected executions; do not guess from supervisor names or assumed daemon parentage. Supervisor-backend executions take the specified loss/stop path. Genuinely qualified, unaffected SH2+ runners can survive controller-only replacement with the same execution ID; this permits neither adoption of a live old supervisor nor omission of any affected execution.

1. **Prepare a compatible pair and a finite window.** Record old/new artifacts, configuration, reader/writer and wire/schema compatibility, affected units, durable obligation inventory, expected stop scope and recovery access. Reconcile unknowns needed for this act. Review the rollback target against every record the new version may retain, not just an old snapshot. Deadline bounds this maintenance attempt, not standing-account lifetime.
2. **Obtain the exact current operator act before its mutating effects.** The operator selects the affected stop scope and confirms its actual content through the protected path. Read-only preparation may precede it; batch scheduling is not blanket authority for later widening or different stops. Persist the admitted maintenance intent and retain original effect IDs and arguments.
3. **Close every applicable admission entry.** Close the applicable caller/autostart/recovery paths and hold the receiver create/launch/ingress fences required by the admitted act. Durably reconcile the selected inventory after closure, and preserve exclusive state ownership through swap and health checks. Closing a UI or draining a queue is insufficient.
4. **Stop and reconcile before changing bytes or modes.** Request journaled stops, confirm the applicable account-generation absence and reconcile every required owner. For credential owners, contain all writer descendants and resolve vendor rotation/store outcome separately. A killed child does not prove whether a provider refresh committed. A never-launched generation uses complete no-launch history plus a fresh fenced absence result, never a dummy launch receipt.
5. **Swap only after the required evidence is complete.** Unknown, refused, timed-out or contradictory results defer the change with admission closed. Do not adopt a live old supervisor, erase journals, retire/recreate a standing identity or restore old state to make a binary installable. Compatible maintenance preserves account generations, homes, identity and outstanding export, delivery and retirement obligations.
6. **Recover control, then re-evaluate admission.** The new owner checks compatible state, replay/observation, time, revocation, restore, ALLSTOP and applicable network/service readiness. Reuse retained records to reconcile old effects; a new physical execution needs its own current admitted prepare/start and receipt. Any session restart or native resume also needs its actual current authority and lock check. Unsupported continuations stay stopped.
7. **Reopen explicitly and observe.** Record the operator's completion/reopen evidence and remaining local holds, publish the current readiness view and enable the authorized entry paths last. Successful replacement does not release a watcher freeze, clear ALLSTOP, renew grants or choose a reboot requester.

For **SH1 C1**, first satisfy the accepted tag/private-consumer gate and the October 19 calendar floor, then use an operator maintenance slot with the host's existing controls. Close every affected entry path for the entire transaction: wrappers and their automatic upgrade/start, scheduled/recovery starts, board runners and operator entry points. Retain the replacement guard and exclusive state ownership through snapshot, swap, start and health verification. The paired release manifest names board/module/wire/goal/storage identities and permitted readers/writers; disposable acceptance covers late old-client starts and all four old/new wrapper/daemon pairings. Reopen only with the paired identity and one verified writer. Rollback must preserve every later retained record; otherwise defer or fix forward. Quiescence cannot be substituted with new platform protocols that do not yet exist.

Today's `tb-sessiond` owns Claude PTYs. Replacing it interrupts those sessions; preserving SH1 behavior does not imply SH2 session survival. Before the window, enumerate live sessions and plan their supported stop/restart or deferral under the actual current runtime. Do not advertise future SH2 consent/recovery semantics as an existing SH1 feature.

## Rollback, restore and emergency stop are different operations

| Situation | Evidence required to continue | State that remains held when evidence is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New binary fails after writing a new record | Old binary can read, safely preserve and write compatible successors to **all retained records**, including later effects, revocations, cleanup/quotas, tombstones, time/epoch evidence and obligations; perform the same fenced stop/absence and compatible swap | Keep admission closed and fix forward if compatibility is absent. An old database/home snapshot is not a rollback mechanism. |
| Restoring a backup predating an acknowledged revocation | External restore fence installed before old bytes become visible; autostart and every direct ledger reader gated; complete surviving revocation/freshness history outside the restored failure domain, or explicit invalidation of old roots and fresh decisions/reissue; independent time/effect/vendor-outcome reconciliation | Diagnostic/observation-only recovery; verdict unavailable and material/admission closed. A witness restored with the same snapshot proves no freshness. Unattributed orphan state remains held for an explicit disposition. |
| Restore completes while ALLSTOP remains recorded | Durable restore reconciliation and the independent complete ALLSTOP recovery act are both satisfied | Clearing the restore mark cannot clear ALLSTOP. Neither service restart nor watcher release can do so. No old root remains as a transitional bypass. |
| ALLSTOP while dispatcher or broker is unavailable | Direct authenticated per-receiver delivery of the same act, root create/launch/ingress fencing, prompt independent containment/stops and broker final material-release fence; later same-act reconciliation of each required service's journaled part | Valid stops can progress without a broker acknowledgement. Missing dispatcher/broker parts prevent **whole-act completion and reopening**, not independent narrowing. Never create an offline writer of dispatcher state. |
| ALLSTOP races queued credential issuance, refresh or a start | Durable freeze checked after asynchronous work and before final material release; fences stop new physical starts/ingress; exact-generation inventory and absence reconcile all in-flight effects | Late material or launch results cannot reopen. Already delivered provider tokens keep their vendor lifetime; local freeze is not vendor revocation. Unknown launch history is not evidence of “never started.” |
| Planned writer-owner replacement after ambiguous refresh | Platform-qualified descendant containment/absence **and** durable vendor-attempt/store/outcome resolution | No blind refresh retry merely because a PID is gone. Codex activation still requires every CB7 replacement/rotation row on both platforms. |

External freshness placement, exact journal/receipt encodings and recovery transaction ordering are publication work, not details an installer may improvise. Preserve complete acknowledgements or explicitly replace the affected authority roots; no finite local timestamp can detect a whole-machine rollback by itself. Broker revocation reconciliation does not also solve an ambiguous vendor rotation.

Bind the actual backup/restore entry points to this procedure before deployment: ordinary backup creation is permitted, but restoring live platform state must not bypass the external fence or startup checks. Qualify broker, ledger and dispatcher restore, including interruption between fencing, reconciliation and reopening; state the whole-host restore boundary explicitly. Reboot acceptance also composes active static-deny precedence, trusted-time recovery, lost/absence reconciliation and current admission. A stopped or pending standing allocation acquires no automatic fresh start authority merely because the machine rebooted.

## Daily operation, park and unpark

Keep ordinary authority horizons visible independently of account lifetime. A standing account is not forcibly retired at a maintenance deadline or lease expiry. `E-rebind` needs current narrowed authority and preserves the account generation, but replaces only the credential binding it names. It does not authorize a session successor, release a watcher hold or recover ALLSTOP. A successfully exec'd env-channel client no longer exists to renew or report; uninterrupted handoff needs its own qualified holder/channel. Otherwise use an explicit supported stop/restart, without claiming continuity or recalling already delivered tokens.

Qualify narrowing and widening separately. At v0, state the actual availability, stop/hold and observer behavior; do not claim v1 per-account egress freeze. At v1, a later narrowing dominates delayed re-apply, closes the applicable proxy sessions and survives replacement/reboot. Release requires its own current authorized widening and remains separate from restarting work. Budget consumption belongs to the account generation/job across listener, binding or service replacement: unknown remaining allowance refuses instead of resetting the day. Telemetry attribution and R-TIME rollover are part of this recovery proof.

Carry the installed verifier into daily operation: at every service start and periodically, verify the range rule and active test-UID precedence, parent-anchor placement/enabled state, enabled service rows, kernel states, served listener/policy generation and resolver restrictions. Missing, mismatched or unreadable evidence drops affected enforced readiness, holds new authority and enters the published report/recovery/stop path under R-MAINT/R-FATE; it does not authorize an implicit unit-wide kill. Publish the root-controlled schedule, bounded entry point and response ordering before activating the claim. The detection window remains explicit. Disposable negative suites do not certify a changing live deployment, and this bounded deployed verifier is not permission to move destructive qualification to the operator host.

Operator work should be able to continue through a delayed rollout: retain the existing board entry until C1, run heavy qualification off-host, stagger maintenance by actual affected set and leave protected observer/recovery access available. Ordinary resource pressure should lead to bounded admission/park decisions, not erased evidence, silent authority extension or account recreation. Output/evidence budgets, unknown-delivery fences and protected control reserves attach to the first actual durable mailbox/host consumer, including an earlier S0b seam if it exposes that behavior. For SH2+, quotas include retained records across replacement so a noisy producer cannot block a stop or another session's durable control. For this review quorum, PARK means its documented handoff/wait discipline; it is not a platform account-retirement or cleanup authorization.

Before enabling automatic liveness recovery, publish and qualify a provider-throttling policy that prevents successor storms. This is proposed implementation policy: SH7 specifies wedge/park semantics, not a generic 429/reset protocol. Distinguish recognized quota incidents from authentication, transport and ambiguous-delivery failures. Unknown reset or non-application evidence stays held; guessed wall time or an arbitrary 429 cannot authorize retry. Verify no duplicate platform delivery or unbounded successor churn, with status/stop still available and current ordinary consent/authority required for resumption. Do not promise prevention of every provider-side context resend or impose a standing-session lifetime cap.

## Workspace and remote-job obligations across a window

Before any workspace-capable allocation is admitted, freeze the cleanup-only consent, exact base/result/manifest contracts and immutable refusal/disposition path. Export after ordinary work expiry is allowed only under the published cleanup authority; revocation, restore, maintenance and ALLSTOP retain their separate gates. A watcher account freeze may permit a qualified cleanup exporter under that authority, whereas node ALLSTOP holds physical starts. Repair is work and requires fresh work authority. Every materialize, work, export or repair execution has its own receipt.

Zero egress does not exclude incoming SSH processes. Close incoming creation/authentication as part of the workplace's published materialize/job/export/replacement/stop lifecycle before claiming account-wide quiet absence. Temporary ingress suspension and permanent retirement/certificate revocation are different transitions; do not kill a relay then leave an authenticated replacement path open.

An export obligation is discharged only once the result and **all referenced content** are durably outside the child's deletion path. A pointer or manifest alone is insufficient. Retain that custody until import acknowledgement or an explicitly authorized disposition; once custody is complete, home retirement need not wait for the eventual board import. A deterministic export refusal is an immutable result, not successful cleanup. Confirmed process absence can release execution capacity while live allocation, UID/home and export obligations remain charged. Bounded fixup requires fresh work authority; current per-act retention/waiver is the default. Standing automatic discard is an additional owner decision, not an implied optimization.

Preserve candidate/job identities and pending obligations across replacement. An old J1 result for C1, whether pass or red, resolves J1 history only: it neither satisfies nor regresses C2 and never permits a forced update of a moved protected ref. Qualify equal-tree/different-commit replies, duplicates and a crash between durable result recording and progress mutation. At consumption, match the requested C/tree/B/H/artifact/recipe/image and the current candidate/request without inventing a CAS wire format. The selected non-agent performer checks authority and the binding for each distinct effect: **CI-free pending publication and runner/artifact handoff may precede candidate acceptance**; protected landing additionally needs current exact-C acceptance/verification and a fresh protected-ref check. Ordinary base refresh creates a new candidate requiring fresh exact-C review and applicable verification; no equal-tree verdict carryover. A runner outage leaves the job pending/held; it does not relocate candidate execution onto an agent account or the busy operator host. The proposed transfer r6 retry is infrastructure-failure only, at most once, never a genuine test red; its publication and qualification are still work. Historical result retention, completion reconciliation and refusal to consume a stale result for the current candidate belong in the same maintenance inventory as process state.

Before the first donor, name the owner and cost of the separate public join-entry host and its existing TLS ingress (CIP-0009 D9); it cannot be colocated with untrusted workplaces. The join point holds no admission-signing key. Keep host/user CA custody outside the workplace/relay as specified by D12. Count that shared readiness and operation once before forecasting donor capacity; this placement requirement does not choose every repository/runner transport topology.
