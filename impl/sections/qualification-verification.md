## 1. Qualify a specific claim at the boundary that enforces it

Keep contract publication, implementation completion, disposable qualification and target-host readiness as separate recorded states. A proposed schema, a passing parser, a successful `fw.apply` receipt, a source review or a green generic CI job does not establish the next state. Unsupported, skipped, stale, partial and unknown evidence cannot be counted as a pass.

Each enabled capability has a versioned evidence packet containing:

- The owning contract revision and its producer/consumer agreement; implementation and qualification-tool commits; canonical-vector version and selected capability scope.
- Actual host/OS release, kernel/backend and image identity; approved binary digest, harness build and entrypoint; effective profile, policy and relevant guard/surface digests. OS names alone are not capability evidence.
- Relevant principal, account generation, execution/controller epoch, effect/request identity and B/C/H/artifact identities. Evidence must bind to the subject whose gate it satisfies.
- A passing positive control, the required negative cases, observed outcomes and retained obligations, crash/restart cuts, and named mutations where a security predicate is tested. A mutant is killed by the intended assertion with a passing baseline, not merely by a compile error or unrelated failure.
- Bounded, secret-free artifacts and an independent review identifying exactly what was proved, what was not exercised, and which changes require requalification. Caps, deadlines and retention limits come from the published contract; this plan invents none.

Qualification is cumulative only within its stated scope. Reuse common encoders, vectors, fixtures and collection tools; re-run affected rows when their contract, implementation, runtime tuple or enforcement inputs change. A successful transport probe does not qualify a model client, hooks, tool children or another entrypoint. Network report `359365e` and historical Codex renewal evidence are pinned observations with those limits.

The implementation owner produces evidence; a reviewer outside that change's implementation lane checks the actual artifacts and refusal behavior; infrastructure owns the disposable environment; operations verifies the installed target tuple and start-time readiness. This review separation is a proposed delivery practice, not a new runtime authorization role. Operator decisions still own policy choices and activation scope.

## 4. Reusable adversarial and recovery corpus

### Authority, observation and emergency closure

Use one versioned grant/revocation canonical and mutation corpus at every verifier, then exercise actual caller/peer checks at the consuming socket. Wrong issuer-parent chain, depth, action/scope, target, generation, principal, expiry and canonical content must fail at the effect boundary. Compare the complete canonical unsigned signed-intent content for an existing `(principal, id)`; adding a digest to the replay key must not turn changed intent into a new request.

Run crashes before durable intent, after intent/before effect, during effect, after result persistence/before acknowledgement and during recovery. Retry/observe the same effect and arguments. A terminal old success reports current removal/retirement; missing history cannot become `not_recorded`; read permission after work authority expires grants no mutation. Include competing starts, retirement, UID reuse and old callback completions. Keep slow cross-service I/O outside the state lock and revalidate under the owning fence before commit.

S0a ALLSTOP qualification includes a dispatcher that is absent/unready and a healthy provisioned generation that never launched. One authenticated act starts the independent cuts/stops without waiting for another component's response; whole-act completion waits for all journaled parts, including dispatcher reconciliation. Establish a generation-bound root create/start/ingress frontier before quiet absence can be final. Never synthesize a launch receipt, start a dummy job, issue a generic UID kill or write another service's offline state. Missing history, incomplete stop or partial recovery holds admission. Delivered provider tokens retain their vendor lifetime.

Restore tests keep the external restore mark, acknowledged revocations, time watermark and elapsed-removal evidence outside the restored failure domain. An old parseable snapshot never opens authorization. Reopen is last and requires both restore reconciliation and independent ALLSTOP recovery; clearing either fence alone does not clear the other. Test boot/clock steps, suspend/resume, lost time confidence and genuine expiry without silently reviving rights. Stops and authorized narrowing remain available.

### Enforcement, budgets and watchers

The helper's dedicated test-UID probes must test precedence against pre-existing quick matches/anchors, loops and tunnels at install, each service start and on schedule. Each lab negative has an exact endpoint/address-family/protocol/operation control and an observation that distinguishes policy denial from DNS outage, absent listener, dead provider or an unexecuted probe. Prove an existing socket carries traffic before narrowing it; exercise the actual system resolver path, not only account packet counters. Never disable containment on an admitted production host to manufacture a positive control. Record the schedule and observed detection window; periodic verification is not a continuous-enforcement claim.

R-NETBIND vectors cover initial generation/listener handoff, enforce-before-active, failure/rollback and post-I/O revalidation. Retain these from the withdrawn provisioning candidate only as existing F-27 qualification; invent no prepared state and do not count a withdrawn finding as new work.

Use one dial-guard and conformance-vector set across N15/N16, covering DNS answers, rebinding, redirects, IPv4/IPv6 and transition ranges. CONNECT qualifies tunnel authority/address admission; it cannot prove an unobserved inner HTTP origin, provider-executed web behavior or absence of exfiltration. No TLS interception is introduced. Near-budget-cap reconnect, service restart, crash, policy reapply and E-rebind keep conservative identity/window usage. Usage state is separate from configuration; durable reservations or unknown/closed recovery are valid designs. Publish the chosen window semantics without conflating calendar and rolling windows; this plan mandates neither rolling-24-hour accounting nor per-byte fsync.

Attribute each observation to the correct actor: direct-fallback refusal needs the actual agent/model-client attempt and kernel evidence, not the proxy's own failed dial. Distinguish unreachable undeclared sockets from denied roles on intentionally reachable declared sockets. N15 attributes accepted SSH to sshd, so egress denial cannot discharge workplace ingress quiescence. Include another unaffected identity when testing per-account freeze and budget scope. Positive marker controls for candidate hooks/scripts must establish that the negative fixture would actually execute without the claimed boundary.

The v1 watcher freeze dominates stale reapply, survives the specified failures and tears down established streams. Qualify the actual `fw.apply`/`fw.remove` observation/effect mapping after publication. Sample generation/boot/interval before acting; stale UID samples cannot target a replacement. Freeze release is not launch authority. Journaled stop and status do not depend on alert delivery. If a stage is status-only, publish that limitation and the authenticated observer detection path. When alerts are enabled, qualify bounded typed content, rate/storage limits, route delivery or recorded failure, and no alert-to-operator-act escalation.

Host ceilings, process visibility, socket reachability and shared-service inventory are actual per-host rows: hard/soft/absent/unknown, with admission minima and any explicit owner acceptance of soft rows. Do not infer Linux admission or macOS exclusion from an OS name. For metadata visibility, first publish readers/phases and the chosen observable boundary, then qualify the complete child/relay/service-mediated view; no blind deny of the root launcher's pre-drop ledger/receipt work, and no claim of total topology secrecy.

## 6. SH1, host lifecycle and optional capabilities

SH1 acceptance preserves the approved source freeze and real board behavior, including provider registrations, goals, completion/rollback binding, notices and machine-local policy. Follow lens 3's F/O/G/X/Y/Z gates: a generic synthetic fixture or a prototype consumer build/vet cannot replace unchanged-test projection, immutable-tag whole-board consumption and extracted-module conformance. Mutations must exercise real converters and policy-denial paths. Preserve the informative Linux baseline distinction and explicit unexecuted opt-ins. C1 remains no earlier than October 19 and also requires its maintenance and compatibility gates; disposable SH2 qualification need not wait for that production window.

H acceptance separately exercises retained-output debt across execution replacement/retirement, aggregate quotas and bounded buffers, and control/evidence reserve sufficient for the specified stop/fence/recovery obligations. Ordinary log rotation is not that proof; no resilience to arbitrary total disk loss is claimed. Unknown delivery remains unknown/fenced unless the published evidence actually proves absence; never automatically replay unknown data. Status/stop remain reachable within the existing bounded recovery contract. Full signed-intent replay and unknown post-dispatch delivery are separate cases.

Use dispatcher-owned account-wide stop and a fresh runner's separate native-lock check. D-RESUME allows the settled same-machine non-breaking semver path without an extra version-change qualification row; retain ordinary launch identity, surface, authority and isolation checks, report `resume-failed` to the caller and add no automatic fallback. Goal authorship stays caller/board; self-goals remain deferred. Do not silently choose the still-open automatic reboot requester.

Ordinary successors share the owner's cumulative or first-start-anchored restart budget, grant window and allocation lifetime. Execution/controller replacement or provider interruption cannot reset those limits; exhaustion refuses another successor. Qualify that trace through the actual host/dispatcher path once the budget encoding is published (SH7:196; DP7:138/596/613). Standing executions retain their distinct no-runtime-limit rule; this adds no standing deadline.

Before enabling any proposed provider-limit automatic recovery, publish its reason/readiness contract and qualify current-epoch classified signals, stale reset hints, repeated limits and expiry while waiting. No reset opens explicit park/stop or a security fence, no limit creates successor churn, and neither limit nor recovery converts an unknown receipt to not-applied. Status/stop remain available. Lens 7's row is a proposed acceptance requirement, not an existing shipped grammar or proof that vendor-side context resends are impossible. Goal-free H remains independent of optional goal clients and board adapters.

Optional web, alerts, generic delegated actions, Muse subscription, keeper automation and donor B each have their own gate. Default-off capability is an honest bounded stage, not evidence of successful qualification. Muse C7 priority is decided; channel and qualification remain proposed/open. Donor B waits for its separate provisioner and coherent harness/bridge/provider/refresh/control principal table. No new project-helper donor verbs, implicit loopback exemption, project-side donor credentials or software key downgrade is inferred.
